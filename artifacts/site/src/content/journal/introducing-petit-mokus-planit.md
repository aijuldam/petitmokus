**In brief:** Petit Mokus PlanIt is a free web app from Petit Mokus that helps families and groups of friends plan a get-together in one private, shared place. The organiser creates a plan with the dates and the place, shares one link in the family chat, and everyone can see the details and choose what they will take care of: a dinner, looking after a child, walking the dog or bringing something. PlanIt is available at [planit.petitmokus.com](https://planit.petitmokus.com/). There is nothing to install, and every Petit Mokus product is free.

## More shared moments, together

A weekend with the grandparents. A summer house with three families. A birthday that brings cousins together from different countries. These are the moments families remember, and the ones that often take the most organising.

Usually it starts in a group chat. Someone asks for the address again. Someone else is not sure who is cooking on Saturday. Questions about who sleeps where get lost under photos and voice notes. And, almost always, one person ends up chasing everyone else.

Petit Mokus PlanIt makes getting together easier for families and friends. It gets everyone on the same page, without endless messages or chasing replies.

## What is Petit Mokus PlanIt?

PlanIt is a planning space for a single get-together: a family holiday, a weekend away, a reunion or any stay where several people and households share a place for a few days.

Each plan brings together everything people need to know, free for the organiser and for everyone invited:

- The dates and the address
- The house or accommodation, with photos
- Who is coming, including children and pets
- Where everyone sleeps
- What needs doing each day, and who is taking care of it
- Updates and conversation, shared only with the people in the plan

PlanIt is for families and groups of friends who want to spend more time together. It is not a public event page or a ticketing tool; each plan is a private space for the people taking part.

## Why PlanIt

### Everyone on the same page

One private place for the dates, address, house, sleeping arrangements and updates, so nobody has to search old messages or ask the organiser again.

### Everyone plays their part

See what needs doing each day and who’s taking care of it. Volunteer for dinner, looking after the little one, walking the dog or bringing something, so it doesn’t all fall on the same person.

### More room to enjoy being together

When meals, care and beds are sorted before you arrive, everyone has time for what they enjoy: their sport, a walk, an outing or simply relaxing, with fewer last-minute negotiations.

## How it works

1. **Create a plan with the dates and the place.** The organiser adds the essentials: when, where and a few words to welcome everyone.
2. **Share the link in your family chat.** One link is enough. Each person opens the plan in their own language.
3. **Everyone sees the plan and picks what they’ll take care of.** Daily responsibilities, such as cooking, tidying shared spaces or caring for a particular child or pet, are clear for everyone to see.

Organisers stay in control. They decide what needs doing, can ask someone to take on a responsibility, and can add co-organisers to share the work. Organisers can also choose how people join: anyone with the link, using just their name, or with a Petit Mokus account.

## Who PlanIt is for

- **Families** organising holidays, long weekends or celebrations across several generations
- **Parents** who want to share childcare and meals fairly during time away
- **Groups of friends** renting a house together
- **Multicultural and long-distance families** who gather from different countries and prefer to read the plan in their own language

## Part of the Petit Mokus family

Petit Mokus makes calm, thoughtful digital experiences for families. It is multicultural at its core, created by a French and Hungarian family in Amsterdam. *Mokus* means squirrel in Hungarian: a small, curious animal that thrives through play, exploration and a love of small, meaningful moments.

Like a little squirrel gathering nuts, Petit Mokus gathers gentle interactions that help families feel more connected, one small moment at a time. Each product supports a different moment in family life:

- **[Petit Mokus Kids](/en/kids/)**: sounds, lullabies and simple games to enjoy together
- **[Petit Mokus BabyNaming](https://babynaming.petitmokus.com/)**: choose your baby’s name together, as two parents
- **[Petit Mokus Wishes](/en/wishes/)**: a wish list for every celebration, from a birth to a birthday or a baptism, shared with one link so nobody buys the same gift twice
- **[Petit Mokus PlanIt](https://planit.petitmokus.com/)**: plan a get-together with family and friends

PlanIt uses the same Petit Mokus account as the other products, so one sign-in works across the family of experiences. Every Petit Mokus product is free to use.

PlanIt and Wishes also work well side by side. Planning a birthday weekend or a family celebration? Use PlanIt to organise the stay, the meals and who sleeps where, and share a Wishes list so loved ones know which gifts are still available.

Little moments matter. PlanIt is there for the bigger ones too: the days when everyone finally comes together.
