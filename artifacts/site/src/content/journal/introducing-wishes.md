A baby on the way. A birthday around the corner. A family gathering with something special to celebrate.

Different moments, often followed by the same question:

“What would you like?”

Sometimes you know exactly what would be useful. Sometimes your child has mentioned something a dozen times, just never when someone asks. And sometimes you’re the one looking for a gift, hoping to find something that feels right.

Meet [Wishes](/en/wishes/), the free wishlist from Petit Mokus. A place to bring gift ideas together, share them with family and friends, and make giving a little easier, for every age and all kinds of happy occasions.

## For a new arrival, and everything that comes after

Preparing to welcome a baby brings plenty of excitement, along with a growing list of things to think about. When loved ones ask how they can help, a shared wishlist gives you somewhere to put the things you would genuinely appreciate.

But Wishes isn’t just for the beginning of family life.

It’s for first birthdays and tenth birthdays. For teenagers with very particular tastes. For parents, partners and grandparents, too. For Christmas, a housewarming, an anniversary or another happy milestone worth marking.

Because family life doesn’t stand still, and a wishlist shouldn’t be something you outgrow.

## A thoughtful gift starts with a little understanding

Giving a gift is a way of saying, “I’m thinking of you.”

A wishlist doesn’t have to take that thoughtfulness away. It gives it a starting point.

Perhaps there’s a book they’ve been hoping to read, something for a favourite hobby, or a practical item that would make everyday life easier. Knowing what someone would enjoy helps you choose with confidence, while leaving the personal touches to you.

For the person sharing their wishes, it also makes answering “What would you like?” feel simpler. No need to remember every idea on the spot or send a different answer to everyone who asks.

## Less back-and-forth, fewer duplicate gifts

Gift ideas have a habit of getting scattered across messages, conversations and half-remembered hints.

Wishes brings them into one place.

Create a list for yourself, your child or an occasion. Add your gift ideas, then share the list with the people you want to include. Guests can reserve a gift, helping everyone coordinate without having to organise a separate conversation about who is getting what.

Less “Has someone already bought this?”

Less searching through old messages.

More confidence that your gift will be welcome.

## For the happy moments, big and small

Not every celebration is a major milestone. Sometimes it’s simply a birthday lunch, a festive weekend or a gathering that brings the people you love around the same table.

Wishes is there when gift-giving is part of the occasion. Not to make the list longer or the celebration bigger, but to make it easier to share ideas and choose something meaningful.

A list is a helping hand, not an expectation. The occasion is still about the person and the people celebrating with them.

## Another little helping hand from Petit Mokus

At Petit Mokus, we want to make a little more room for the good parts of family life: discovering, playing, preparing and spending time together.

Wishes brings that same spirit to giving. A simple way to make one small part of a celebration easier, from welcoming a new baby to celebrating someone who has been part of your life for years.

Whatever the occasion, the idea is the same: less time sorting out the details, more room to enjoy the moment.
