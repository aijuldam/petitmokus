BabyNaming now carries the Petit Mokus name, logo and colours, and lives at [babynaming.petitmokus.com](https://babynaming.petitmokus.com).

It is still the same tool for two parents choosing a baby name together. Petit Mokus is now the family name for both of our products: Petit Mokus Kids and Petit Mokus BabyNaming.
